Sow after the first consistent autumn rains (cumulative >25mm). Ensure soil temperature >10°C at 5cm depth.

January – April

Tillering and stem elongation phases. Apply split nitrogen applications (e.g. ammonium nitrate) during active tillering.

{% trans "Critical Risk" %} warning
{% trans "Critical Care Window" %}
March – April

Heading and grain filling. Water deficit or heat waves (Sirocco) during this window causes severe yield collapse.

{% trans "Stage 4" %} agriculture
{% trans "When to Harvest" %}
25 May – 20 June

Harvest when grain moisture drops to 12–14%. Avoid harvesting during extreme noon heat to minimize shatter loss.
